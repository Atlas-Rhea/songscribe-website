Harmony

The Harmony card shows the chords that belong together in your song's key, so you can hear them and find your next chord.

On this page
  • See the chords in your key
  • Hear a chord and see what comes next
  • Switch to 7ths
  • Build a run of chords
  • Visit a related key
  • Add the chords to your song

See the chords in your key

  1. Open the Tools tab and tap the Harmony card.

The first chord of the key sits in the middle. The other six circle around it, each marked with a Roman numeral and a chord name. The card follows the key in your Song Settings.

Hear a chord and see what comes next

  1. Tap a circle. The chord plays and the circle stays highlighted.
  2. Look for the circles with a ring. These are the chords that often come next. After D in the key of G, the rings are on G and Em.
  3. Read the panel below. It shows the chord, its job in the key and its notes. The job reads Home · tonic, Away · predominant or Tension · dominant.

Switch to 7ths

  1. Tap 7ths in the switch above the circles. The circles become seventh chords.
  2. Tap Triads to go back.

Build a run of chords

Each chord you tap goes into a row under the panel, up to eight.

  1. Tap the chords you want, in order.
  2. Tap the play button to hear them one after another.
  3. Tap a chord in the row to take it out, or tap the cross to clear the row.

Visit a related key

The chips under the circles show your song's key and four nearby keys. In the key of G they read G, Em, Gm, C and D.

  1. Tap a chip. The circles change to that key. A line under the chips says what the key is, such as "Em uses the same notes as G, with a sadder feel".
  2. Look for small dots under the chord names. Those chords are also in your song's key.
  3. Tap your song's key to go back.

The row of chords you built stays, so you can mix in a chord from the other key.

Add the chords to your song

  1. Build a row of chords, then tap the button under it. It reads Add chord to song for one chord and Add 4 chords to song for four. For a moment it then reads Added to end of song.
  2. Open the Chords tab and scroll to the end of the song.

The chords sit in order on their own line at the bottom. Drag each one to the word you want.
