Harmony

The Harmony card shows the chords that belong together in your song's key, so you can hear them and find your next chord.

On this page
  • See the chords in your key
  • Hear a chord and see its notes
  • Switch to 7ths
  • Visit a related key
  • Add a chord to your song

See the chords in your key

  1. Open the Tools tab and tap the Harmony card.

The first chord of the key sits in the middle. The other six circle around it, each marked with a Roman numeral and a chord name. The card follows the key in your Song Settings.

Hear a chord and see its notes

  1. Tap a circle. The chord plays.
  2. Scroll the page up if needed. A panel shows the chord, its job in the key (such as Tonic) and its notes.

Switch to 7ths

  1. Tap 7ths below the circles. The circles become seventh chords. The chip now reads Triads.
  2. Tap Triads to go back.

Heads-up

After switching, the panel can still show the old triad. Tap the circle again to refresh it.

Visit a related key

The four chips under the circles move you to a nearby key:

  • Rel is the relative key, which shares the same notes (G major to E minor).
  • Par is the parallel key, same starting note but the opposite mood (G major to G minor).
  • IV is the key a fourth up (G major to C major).
  • V is the key a fifth up (G major to D major).
  1. Tap a chip. The circles change to that key.
  2. Tap Key to return to your song's own key.

Add a chord to your song

  1. Tap a circle, then tap Add to Song. The button reads Added!.
  2. Open the Chords tab.

The app gives no other message. The chord goes on the end of the first chorus line, which can leave it crowded against the chord before it. Drag it to the word you want.
