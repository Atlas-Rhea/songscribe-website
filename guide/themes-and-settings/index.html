Themes and settings

Some steps need Lifetime

Change how SongScribe looks and set the defaults every new song starts with.

On this page
  • Open Settings
  • Pick a theme and font
  • Set your defaults
  • Use a footswitch
  • Reset and get help

Open Settings

Tap the gear at the bottom of the sidebar. Or tap ⋯ in the editor, then Settings.

Pick a theme and font

  1. Under Appearance, tap Theme and tap a swatch. There are 17, all free.
  2. Under Text, tap one of the five fonts: SongScribe, San Francisco, New York, Rounded or Monospace.
  3. Under Editor Text Size, choose Small, Medium or Large.

Every theme has a light and a dark version. They follow your phone's appearance, and there is no light and dark switch inside SongScribe.

Set your defaults

Under Editor and Audio:

  • Default Artist Name fills the artist on new songs.
  • Default Instrument is Guitar, Bass or Ukulele.
  • Left-handed fretboard flips the fretboard.
  • Guitar Tone is Steel String, Nylon Classical or Electric Clean.
  • Lossless recording records takes at full quality.

Lifetime

Lossless recording and Footswitch control need Lifetime or the free trial. Lossless switches off when a trial ends.

Use a footswitch

Under Performance, turn on Footswitch control, then open Configure Footswitch. You can connect a Bluetooth MIDI pedal, and Learn assigns a pedal press to Start / Stop Scrolling, Next Song, Previous Song, Scroll Faster, Scroll Slower or Exit Performance.

Reset and get help

Under System, Reset Settings puts your choices back to the defaults. Delete All Data erases every song, recording, folder and setlist, and it cannot be undone. If you use iCloud, they are removed from your other devices too. Under About, Contact Support writes to support@songscribe.io.
