Your first song in five minutes

Write two lines, put a chord over a word, and record yourself singing it. Everything here is free.

On this page
  • Create a song
  • Type two lines and add a Verse
  • Add a chord
  • Move a chord
  • Record a take
  • Play it back
  • Where next

The first time you open SongScribe, it opens a tutorial song, SongScribe State of Mind, after a short moment. Your own songs start from the hamburger button at the top left.

Create a song

  1. Tap the hamburger button at the top left.
  2. Tap + in the bottom bar of the sidebar.
  3. Type a name in the Song name field and tap Create.

Tip

The six tutorial songs in the sidebar are a good way to explore. Open one and look around before you start your own.

Type two lines and add a Verse

  1. Tap the blank page once, then type two lines of lyrics.
  2. Tap the section button in the bar above the keyboard and choose Verse.

Add a chord

  1. Tap the Chords tab at the bottom.
  2. Tap the word where the chord changes.
  3. Choose a root and quality in the picker.
  4. Tap Insert.

Move a chord

  1. Press and hold a chord pill until it lifts off the line.
  2. Drag it above another word and lift your finger.

Record a take

  1. Tap the Record tab.
  2. Tap the record button at the bottom right and sing or play.
  3. Tap the stop button when you finish.

Play it back

  1. Tap your take, called Take 1, in the list.
  2. Tap the play button.
