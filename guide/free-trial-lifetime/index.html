Free, trial and Lifetime

SongScribe is free to use with two limits. Lifetime removes them for $19.95, paid once. There is no subscription.

Free and Lifetime

Free Lifetime
Songs 10 (tutorial songs don't count) Unlimited
Takes per song 5 Unlimited
Chords, tuner, metronome, harmony Yes Yes
Themes and fonts All All
Setlists and Performance mode Yes Yes
Export No Yes
Layers No Yes
Lossless recording, Footswitch No Yes

Start the 7-day trial

Everything is unlocked for 7 days.

  1. Open the sidebar.
  2. Tap Start Free Trial in the banner at the top of the song list. The upgrade screen opens.
  3. Tap Start Free Trial there.

The banner then counts down the days left and the end date. The same countdown shows at the top of Settings. Three days before the trial ends, SongScribe reminds you.

When the trial ends

Nothing is deleted. Your songs and takes stay, even past the free limits, and layers you recorded keep playing. You just can't create an 11th song or a 6th take on a song until you buy Lifetime. Lossless recording switches off, and comes back when you buy.

Buy Lifetime

  1. Open Settings from the gear in the sidebar.
  2. Tap SongScribe Lifetime.
  3. Tap Get SongScribe Lifetime — $19.95.

You can do this at any time, including during the trial. A gold record celebration plays, tap Start Writing, and the gold record stays at the top of Settings.

Tip

On a new phone, tap Restore Purchases in Settings to get Lifetime back on the same Apple account.
