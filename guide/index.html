SongScribe Guide

Short how-tos for every part of the app, starting with your first song.

Start here

Your first song in five minutes

Create a song, type two lines, place a chord, record a take and play it back, all in your first five minutes with SongScribe.

Songs

  • Songs and folders

    Open the sidebar, create a song, sort your songs into folders, pin favorites and move or delete several at once.

  • Import songs you already have

    Bring in song files or pasted lyrics, with chords, sections, title, key and tempo carried over.

Writing

  • Lyrics and sections

    Write lyrics, format them, divide a song into Verse and Chorus sections, rearrange them, and keep notes with each song.

  • Adding and moving chords

    Put chords above the exact word they belong to, change or remove them, and drag them to a new spot.

  • Chord shapes

    Build any chord in the picker, browse its guitar or piano shapes, hear it, and find a chord by tapping out the frets.

  • Key, capo and tuning

    Set a song's instrument, find or change its key, move every chord up or down, add a capo and choose a tuning.

Recording

  • Recording a take

    Capture an idea in one tap, keep writing while it records, and rename or delete takes. Free songs hold five takes.

  • Listening back and markers

    Play a take, scrub, slow it down without changing pitch, loop a passage and drop markers on the spots you want to remember.

  • Trim and crop a take

    Cut the silence from the ends of a take with two handles, and see what happens to your markers before anything is changed.

  • LayersLifetime

    Record a second part over a take, balance each layer with its own volume slider, and mix them down into one take.

Tools

  • Metronome and tempo

    Set a tempo from 40 to 240 BPM, tap it in, pick a time signature and a click sound, and keep the beat going while you read your lyrics.

  • Harmony

    See the seven chords of your song's key, hear each one, switch to 7ths, jump to related keys and send a chord into your song.

  • Tuner

    Tune any instrument or your voice with a live note name, cents readout and a droplet that settles in the middle when you are in tune.

Playing live

  • Setlists and Performance modeSome steps need Lifetime

    Line up songs for a gig, save the set, then perform it full screen with auto-scroll and a speed control.

Sharing and setup

  • Export and shareLifetime

    Turn a song into a PDF, ChordPro, ASCII or plain text file, or a SongScribe Project, and send it with AirDrop, Mail or Files.

  • Themes and settingsSome steps need Lifetime

    Pick a theme and font, set your defaults, and find the audio, footswitch and reset controls in Settings.

  • Free, trial and Lifetime

    What SongScribe gives you for free, what Lifetime adds for a one-time $19.95, and how the 7-day trial works.

  • Sync and FAQ

    How SongScribe works offline, what iCloud carries between devices, and quick answers to common questions.
