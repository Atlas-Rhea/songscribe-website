Export and share

Lifetime

Make a printable chart or a file for another app, check it in a live preview, and send it from your phone.

On this page
  • Open the export sheet
  • Choose a format
  • Shape the PDF
  • Share it

Lifetime

Export needs SongScribe Lifetime or the free trial. Without either, Export has a padlock and opens the upgrade screen.

Open the export sheet

  1. Open the song you want to export.
  2. Tap ⋯ at the top right of the editor.
  3. Tap Export.

The preview at the top redraws as you change anything below it.

Choose a format

Tap Format and pick one.

  • PDF: a chart to print or share.
  • ChordPro: chords in brackets inside the lyrics, a standard that other chord apps read.
  • ASCII: chords above the words in plain characters.
  • Plain Text: lyrics only.
  • SongScribe Project: the whole song in one file. Opening it brings the song back into SongScribe.

Shape the PDF

  1. Under Display Style, choose Lyric, Busker or Musician. Musician draws chords as cards.
  2. Set Columns to Single or two.
  3. Set Font Size with the minus and plus buttons, or tap Auto to fit the song on the fewest pages.
  4. Pick a Page Size.
  5. Under Options, switch Section Headings on or off.

Share it

  1. Tap the share button at the top right of the export sheet.
  2. Choose AirDrop, Mail, Save to Files or any other destination in the iOS share sheet.
