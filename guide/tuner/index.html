Tuner

Play a note and see how far it is from perfect pitch. Tune until the droplet rests in the center.

On this page
  • Open the tuner
  • Tune a string
  • Choose your instrument
  • The tuner only listens when it is open

Open the tuner

  1. Open the Tools tab and tap the Tuner card.
  2. If your device asks to use the microphone, tap Allow.

The card says Listening... until it hears a note.

Tune a string

  1. Play one note and let it ring.
  2. Read the card. It shows the note name, how many cents you are off, and the frequency in Hz.
  3. A droplet on the gauge shows the direction. Left of center means flat, so tune up. Right of center means sharp, so tune down.

When you are within 3 cents, the droplet slides to the center and the readout turns green.

Choose your instrument

  1. Tap the instrument button at the left of the card. It reads All until you choose.
  2. Pick Chromatic, Guitar, Bass, Vocal or Violin.

Chromatic listens for any note. The other choices listen only in that instrument's range, which helps it ignore stray sounds.

Tip

In a noisy room, try the NS button beside the instrument button. It switches noise suppression on and off.

Heads-up

As a note fades away, the reading can swing to a neighboring note. Judge the pitch while the note is still ringing.

The tuner only listens when it is open

The microphone is on while the Tuner card is open. It stops when you close the card, leave the Tools tab or move SongScribe to the background.
