Setlists and Performance mode

Some steps need Lifetime

Build a set, save it, and read your chords and lyrics hands-free on stage. Setlists and Performance mode are free.

On this page
  • Build a setlist
  • Save, load and clear
  • Perform

Build a setlist

  1. Tap the hamburger button at the top left of the editor to open the sidebar.
  2. Tap Setlist at the top.
  3. Tap songs to add them. Numbered badges show the running order.

To reorder, press and hold a row in the Setlist Order card, then drag it up or down. The numbers update. Nothing on screen hints at this, but it works.

Save, load and clear

  1. In Setlist mode, tap ⋯ in the sidebar.
  2. Choose Save Setlist, type a name and tap Save.
  1. To bring a set back later, choose Load Setlist and tap its name.
  2. To start over, choose Clear Setlist.

Perform

  1. In Setlist mode, tap the play button at the bottom left of the sidebar. It has no label, and does nothing while the set is empty.
  1. Tap the play button at the bottom to start scrolling.
  2. Drag the bar on the right edge up to scroll faster and down to scroll slower. The range is 0.18× to 2×.

The controls fade once the lyrics are scrolling. Tap the lyrics to bring them back, along with the header, which sits on a clean background above the scrolling words.

Scrolling stops at the end of the song. The screen stays awake the whole time.

  1. Use the fast-forward and rewind buttons to move between songs. The header shows your place, such as 1 of 4. The next song opens from its first line.
5. Tap the **X** at the top left to leave.

Tip

To perform one song, add only that song to the set. You do not need to save it.

Lifetime

To run Performance mode from a footswitch, go to Settings → Performance → Footswitch control. This part needs SongScribe Lifetime.
