Lyrics and sections

Write the words on the Lyrics tab, mark where each verse and chorus starts, and keep rough ideas in a notes page.

On this page
  • Format your words
  • Add a section
  • Rearrange or rename a section
  • Undo and redo
  • Keep notes

Format your words

  1. Tap a line to bring up the keyboard.
  2. Tap Aa in the bar above the keyboard.
  3. Choose Heading, Italic or Bold.

Add a section

  1. Tap the section button in the bar above the keyboard.
  2. Choose Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Intro or Outro.

A badge with the section's name appears above the lines it covers.

Heads-up

In a song that already has lyrics, the new badge can land after the last line instead of at your cursor. Move it with Move Up in the badge's menu (below).

Rearrange or rename a section

  1. Tap a section's badge.
  2. Choose Rename, Duplicate, Move Up, Move Down or Delete.
  3. For Rename, tap a name such as Pre-Chorus, Hook, Instrumental, Breakdown or Refrain, or tap Custom... to type your own.

Duplicate copies the section with its lyrics.

Undo and redo

The undo and redo buttons live in the bar above the keyboard, so tap into the text first to see them. They cover typing, formatting and section changes.

Keep notes

  1. Tap ⋯ at the top right of the editor.
  2. Tap Notes.
  3. Type your ideas, then tap Done.
