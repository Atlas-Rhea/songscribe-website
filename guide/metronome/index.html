Metronome and tempo

Set a tempo and time signature for your song and keep a click running while you write or play. Both are saved with the song.

On this page
  • Set the tempo
  • Tap the tempo in
  • Choose a time signature
  • Change the sound
  • Start, stop and play along

Set the tempo

  1. Open the Tools tab and tap the Metronome & Tempo card.
  2. Pick a tempo from the row of tempo chips, anywhere from 40 to 240 BPM. The row scrolls and centers on the current tempo.

Tap the tempo in

  1. Tap Tap Tempo in time with the music, several times in a row.

SongScribe averages your last few taps and sets the BPM to match.

Choose a time signature

  1. Next to Time, pick the number of beats, from 1 to 12.
  2. Pick the note value: 2, 4, 8 or 16.

Beat one gets a stronger click, so you can hear where each bar starts.

Change the sound

  1. Open the sound menu on the card.
  2. Choose Click, Wood Block, Electronic, Hi-Hat or Cowbell.

Start, stop and play along

  1. Tap the play button on the card to start the click. Tap it again to stop.
  2. Switch to the Lyrics tab. A small floating pill follows you there, with a ball that swings across the beat dots, one swing per beat.
  1. Tap the square in the pill to stop the click. Tap the X to remove the pill.

Heads-up

The pill sits at the top of the lyrics and can cover the first section badge. Tapping the square stops the sound but leaves the pill on screen. Use the X to clear it.

Tip

Your tempo and time signature belong to the song. Open a different song and the card shows that song's settings.
