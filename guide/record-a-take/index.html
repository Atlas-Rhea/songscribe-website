Recording a take

Record a quick idea against the song you are writing, then find it again in a list of takes.

On this page
  • Start a take
  • Pause, or keep writing while it records
  • Stop and find your take
  • Record with the metronome
  • Rename or delete a take

Start a take

  1. Open the song, then the Record tab.
  2. Tap the record button at the bottom right.

A card at the top of the list shows a waveform that reacts to the sound, with low sounds in the middle and high sounds at the edges. Beside it are the timer, the word Recording and a Pause button. The record button turns into a stop square. The waveform listens only while the Record tab is on screen.

Pause, or keep writing while it records

Switch to the Lyrics, Chords or Tools tab and keep working. The take keeps going, and a slim pill sits under the title. From left to right it has a Pause button, the timer, a thin line that moves with how loud the mic hears you, and a Stop button. If the metronome pill is also showing, the recording pill sits directly under it.

  1. Tap Pause on the pill, or on the card on the Record tab. The button becomes Resume, and the card says Paused.
  2. Tap Resume to carry on.

Paused time is not part of the take's length. A phone call pauses the take too; resume it from the pill or the card. Recording still starts only from the record button on the Record tab.

Heads-up

Opening a different song ends the recording.

Stop and find your take

  1. Tap the stop button on the Record tab, or Stop on the pill. Stop saves the take and closes the pill.

Takes are named Take 1, Take 2 and so on, newest at the top. Each row shows its length and a small waveform. A take shorter than one second is thrown away, so an accidental tap leaves nothing behind.

Lifetime

A free song holds 5 takes. Trying to record a 6th opens the upgrade screen. Your existing takes stay put.

Record with the metronome

If the metronome is playing through the speaker, SongScribe asks first with No Headphones Connected. The click would leak into your recording.

  1. Tap Cancel to plug in headphones, or Record Anyway to go ahead.

Rename or delete a take

  1. Open the take.
  2. Tap ⋯ at the top right, then choose Rename or Delete.

You can also swipe a take left in the list to reveal Delete.
