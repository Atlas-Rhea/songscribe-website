Key, capo and tuning

Every song has its own instrument, key, capo and tuning. You set them all in Song Settings.

On this page
  • Choose the instrument
  • Detect the key
  • Transpose a song
  • Use a capo
  • Change the tuning

Choose the instrument

  1. Open a song, tap ⋯ at the top right, then Song Settings.
  2. In Instrument, tap Guitar, Bass, Ukulele, Mandolin or Banjo.

Chord diagrams follow the instrument: four strings for ukulele, bass and mandolin, five for banjo.

Detect the key

  1. Scroll to the Key card.
  2. Tap Detect from chords.

You see a line like Detected G (85% confidence). The percentage varies from song to song.

Transpose a song

  1. In Key, tap the down or up arrow beside Current.
  2. Tap Transpose in the Transpose Chords? alert.

Every chord moves a semitone. The song remembers the key it was Originally in.

Heads-up

The alert says the change cannot be undone from settings. Tap Cancel if you are not sure.

Heads-up

Starting a scroll with your finger on the key wheel can spin it and change the key without asking. Scroll with your finger beside it.

Use a capo

  1. Under Capo & Display, set Capo Fret from 0 to 12.
  2. Read the line below it, which shows both the shape you play and the pitch that sounds, such as G shape (sounds A#).

The chord picker then shows a capo bar and the sounding pitch.

Change the tuning

  1. Scroll to Tuning.
  2. Tap a preset, for example Drop D, or set each string yourself. Swipe the row of presets sideways to see more.
  3. Tap Save Custom Tuning to keep your own and reuse it in other songs.
