Listening back and markers

Open a take to hear it, move around in it, practice it slowly and flag the moments that matter.

On this page
  • Play a take
  • Scrub
  • Change the speed
  • Loop a passage
  • Drop markers
  • Jump to a marker or add a note

Play a take

  1. On the Record tab, tap a take.
  2. Tap the play button. Tap it again to pause.
  3. Use the back and forward buttons beside it to skip 15 seconds.

Scrub

  1. Press the playhead line on the waveform and drag it.

The line lifts slightly while you hold it, so you can see you have it.

Change the speed

  1. Tap the turtle to slow down or the hare to speed up. Each tap moves one step, anywhere from 0.25× to 2×.
  2. Tap the speed label under the controls to return to normal.

Pitch stays the same at every speed.

Loop a passage

  1. Tap Loop. The take starts playing, with handles at both ends.
  2. Drag the handles to the part you want to repeat.
  3. Tap Done to leave loop mode.

Drop markers

  1. Play the take.
  2. Tap Marker at the moment you want to flag. Playback keeps going.

Each marker appears as a line on the waveform and a time chip beneath it.

Jump to a marker or add a note

  1. Tap a chip, or its line, to jump there.
  2. Press and hold a chip to open a menu, then choose Add Note or Delete.

Tip

Swipe the row of chips sideways when there are more than fit on screen.
