Listening back and markers

Open a take to hear it, move around in it, practice it slowly and flag the moments that matter.

On this page
  • Play a take
  • Scrub
  • Change the speed
  • Loop a passage
  • Drop markers
  • Jump to a marker or add a note

Play a take

  1. On the Record tab, tap a take.
  2. Tap the play button. Tap it again to pause. The time reads current / total, for example 0:42 / 2:15.
  3. Use the back and forward buttons beside it to skip 15 seconds.

On a layered take the total is the longest track that is not looping. While a layer is being recorded, only the elapsed time shows.

Scrub

  1. Press the playhead line on the waveform and drag it.

The line lifts slightly while you hold it, so you can see you have it.

Change the speed

  1. Tap the turtle to slow down or the hare to speed up. Each tap moves one step, anywhere from 0.25× to 2×.
  2. Tap the speed label under the controls to return to normal. The label is always there, and reads a dim 1× at normal speed.

Pitch stays the same at every speed.

Loop a passage

  1. Tap Loop. Handles appear at both ends of the waveform, in the theme's second accent color (teal on the default theme), and a hint says Drag the edges to set the loop. Trim stays yellow.
  2. Drag the handles to the part you want to repeat.
  3. Tap Play. The take does not start by itself.
  4. Tap Loop again to leave loop mode.

Heads-up

On a layered take the loop region is not applied to the mix. Entering or leaving Loop stops the mix.

Drop markers

  1. Play the take.
  2. Tap Marker at the moment you want to flag. Playback keeps going.

Each marker appears as a line on the waveform and a time chip beneath it. Adding a note to one marker does not widen the others, and a long note is cut off with "…" so every chip stays within reach.

Jump to a marker or add a note

  1. Tap a chip, or its line, to jump there.
  2. Press and hold a chip to open a menu, then choose Add Note or Delete.

Tip

Swipe the row of chips sideways when there are more than fit on screen.
