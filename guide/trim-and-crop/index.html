Trim and crop a take

Keep only the good part of a take. Cropping is permanent, so SongScribe tells you what it will do to your markers first.

On this page
  • Trim a take
  • Check your markers

Trim a take

  1. Open a take and tap Trim.
  2. Drag the two yellow handles so they surround the part you want to keep. Everything outside them dims.
  3. Tap Crop to cut the take down to that part, or Cancel to leave it as it was.

Heads-up

Crop replaces the recording for good. There is no way to bring back the parts you cut.

Check your markers

If a crop would move or remove markers, you see Crop recording? with a count, for example "2 markers will move. 1 marker will be deleted."

  1. Tap Crop to go ahead, or Cancel to go back to the handles.

Markers inside the part you keep slide to their new time. Markers outside it are deleted.
