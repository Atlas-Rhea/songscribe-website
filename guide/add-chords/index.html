Adding and moving chords

Place a chord over the exact word where you change chords, then fix or move it later.

On this page
  • Add a chord
  • Change or remove a chord
  • Move a chord
  • Switch between lyrics and chords
  • Undo a mistake

Add a chord

  1. Open the Chords tab.
  2. Tap the word where the chord changes.
  3. Choose the root and quality of the chord in the picker.
  4. Tap Insert.

On the Chords tab the keyboard stays hidden and typing is switched off, so the page is just for placing chords.

Tip

The picker lets you build any chord and see its shape. See Chord shapes.

Change or remove a chord

  1. Tap a chord pill.
  2. To change it, pick something else and tap Update.
  3. To remove it, tap the trash button.

The picker opens already set to that chord, including the shape you chose.

Move a chord

  1. Press and hold a chord pill until it lifts off the line.
  2. Drag it to the word it should sit above. A blinking caret shows where it will land.
  3. Lift your finger.

Heads-up

Drop the chord on a word. If you let go in empty space below the lyrics, it stays there and is not attached to any word.

Switch between lyrics and chords

The Lyrics and Chords tabs show the same song. Chords are hidden on the Lyrics tab so you can read the words on their own.

Undo a mistake

The undo and redo buttons are in the bar above the keyboard on the Lyrics tab. Switch to Lyrics, tap into the text, and tap undo. One undo reverses a whole chord drag.
