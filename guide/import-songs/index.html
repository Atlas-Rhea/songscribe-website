Import songs you already have

Bring in songs you wrote elsewhere, from a file or by pasting, without retyping them.

On this page
  • Import from File
  • Paste Lyrics
  • What carries over
  • Getting sections to come through

Import from File

  1. Open the sidebar and tap ⋯, then Import from File.
  2. Browse to your file and tap it, then tap Open.

You can choose .txt, .songscribe and ChordPro files ending in .cho, .chordpro, .chord or .crd.

Paste Lyrics

  1. Open the sidebar and tap ⋯, then Paste Lyrics.
  2. Type a name in Song title (optional).
  3. Paste your song into the large text area.
  4. Tap Import. It stays grey until there is text. You see 1 song imported.

What carries over

  • Chords. A chord in square brackets, like [G] or [Em], becomes a chord above that word.
  • Title, key and tempo. Lines like {title: Paper Lanterns}, {key: G} and {tempo: 92} fill in the song's details.
  • Line breaks. Your lines stay as lines.
  • Sections. See below.

Getting sections to come through

ChordPro block markers become sections. A label after the colon is the section's name; without one, you get Verse, Chorus or Bridge. The short forms {sov}, {soc} and {sob} work too.

{start_of_verse: Verse 1}
[G]Paper lanterns [Em]on the water
{end_of_verse}

A section name alone on a line in square brackets also works:

[Chorus]
[C]Carry me [G]home
