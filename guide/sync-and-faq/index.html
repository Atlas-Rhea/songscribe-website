Sync and FAQ

SongScribe works with no connection and no account. If you use iCloud, your songs follow you to your other devices.

On this page
  • Offline and iCloud
  • Questions
  • What SongScribe doesn&#39;t do

Offline and iCloud

Writing, recording, chords and export all work offline.

SongScribe has no sync switch. It follows the iCloud sign-in on your phone. Songs, folders, setlists, recording details, markers, custom tunings and your settings sync. Recording audio is kept in iCloud Drive when iCloud is on, and you can also see recordings in the Files app. If iCloud isn't available, the app keeps working on the phone.

Questions

Where are my songs? Tap the hamburger button at the top left to open the sidebar. A new install opens a tutorial song first; the rest are in the list.

How do I find a song? Type in the Search songs field at the top of the sidebar list. It matches titles and artists.

Can I use it without an account? Yes. There is nothing to sign up for.

How do I perform just one song? Open the sidebar, switch to Setlist, tap that song to add it, then tap the play button at the bottom left. You don't need to save the setlist.

Where is undo on the Chords tab? It isn't there. Switch to Lyrics, tap into the text, and use undo in the bar above the keyboard.

Can I change tempo or time signature? Yes, in the Metronome card on the Tools tab. They are saved per song.

Does it have a dark mode? Yes. Every theme has one, and it follows your phone's appearance.

What happens if I pass the free limits? Nothing is deleted. See Free, trial and Lifetime.

How do I get help? Email support@songscribe.io, or tap Contact Support at the bottom of Settings.

What SongScribe doesn't do

  • It has no drum machine.
  • There is no Mac or web version.
  • Markers are not included in exported files.
  • Layers can't hold markers.
