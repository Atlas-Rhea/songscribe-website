Songs and folders

Keep all your songs in one place: start new ones, group them in folders, and keep the ones you play most at the top.

On this page
  • Open the sidebar
  • Create a song
  • Find a song
  • Make a folder
  • Move a song into a folder
  • Pin a song
  • Move or delete several songs
  • Delete one song

Open the sidebar

  1. Tap the hamburger button at the top left of the editor.
  2. Make sure the Songs segment is selected (the other one is Setlist).

The six tutorial songs are already there. They do not count toward the free limit of 10 songs.

Create a song

  1. In the sidebar, tap + in the bottom bar.
  2. Type a name in the Song name field. Leave it blank and the song is called Untitled Song.
  3. Tap Create.

Find a song

  1. In the sidebar, tap Search songs at the top of the list.
  2. Type part of a title or an artist name.
  3. Tap the x in the field to clear it and see every song again.

The field is also in the Setlist view.

Make a folder

  1. In the sidebar, tap ⋯, then New Folder.
  2. Type a name in Folder name and tap Create.

Move a song into a folder

  1. Press and hold a song in the sidebar.
  2. Tap Move to Folder.
  3. Choose a folder, or Unfiled to take it out of its folder.

Pin a song

  1. Swipe a song to the right.
  2. Tap the orange Pin button.

Pinned songs sit in their own Pinned section at the top of the list.

Move or delete several songs

  1. In the sidebar, tap ⋯, then Select Songs.
  2. Tap each song you want.
  3. Use the buttons in the bottom bar: the folder button moves them, the trash button deletes them, and the close button leaves selection mode.

The trash button asks you to confirm before it deletes.

Delete one song

  1. Swipe the song to the left.
  2. Tap Delete.
  3. In the alert, tap Delete to confirm, or Cancel to keep the song. It asks Delete “<title>”? and says this can't be undone.
