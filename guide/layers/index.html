Layers

Lifetime

Sing a harmony or play a second part while you hear the first, then balance the parts and mix them into one take.

On this page
  • Record a layer
  • Balance the layers
  • Mix down to one take

Lifetime

Layers need SongScribe Lifetime or the free trial. Without either, the Layer button is locked and opens the upgrade screen.

Record a layer

  1. Open a take and tap Layer.
  2. If you are not wearing headphones, No Headphones Connected warns that the take will play through the speaker and may leak into your new layer. Tap Record Anyway or Cancel.
  3. Play or sing along. The play button becomes a stop square while you record.
  4. Tap the stop square to finish.

A take can hold up to 4 layers.

Balance the layers

The base take and each layer appear as stacked waveforms, Base and Layer 1, each with its own volume slider. One master slider sets the overall volume.

Mix down to one take

  1. Tap Downmix.
  2. Choose Keep to hold on to the original layers, or Discard to remove them.

The mix is saved before you answer. With Keep, a new take such as Take 1 (Mix) appears in your list beside the layered one.
